## Upstream Source Data Licensing

The CC-BY 4.0 license applies to the compilation and metadata generated by GovDataHub. The underlying primary datasets harvested from external endpoints remain governed by their respective original licenses. Every dataset in this catalog carries an allowlisted license. Datasets whose license could not be verified are **excluded**, not guessed.

When re-using or republishing individual datasets or records, you must comply with the upstream source attribution requirements outlined below.

## Attribution Requirements

### 1. GovDataHub Compilation (This Repository)

> Contains curated data and metadata compiled by GovDataHub, licensed under [CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Include the statement above (or a link to this repository) when using or republishing this database compilation.

### 2. Open Government Licence v3.0 (data.gov.uk datasets)

> Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

Include the statement above (or a link to [https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/)) wherever you republish these catalogs or underlying records.

### 3. Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY)

Attribute the original publisher named in the `publisher` column of each dataset row.

### 4. US Public Domain (17 U.S.C. § 105)

No copyright restriction in the United States. No attribution is legally required for upstream source data originating from US federal agencies, though crediting the source agency is good practice.

Rows labeled `license_source = inferred-federal` were classified by authorship (federal agency publisher or federal domain), not by an explicit license field in the source API. The underlying legal basis is 17 U.S.C. § 105 (works of US federal government employees). Contractor-produced data is excluded from this inference by design.
