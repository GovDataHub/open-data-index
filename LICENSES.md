# GovDataHub — License & Attribution Register

## License policy

Every dataset in this catalog carries an allowlisted license. Datasets whose
license could not be verified are **excluded**, not guessed.

## Attribution requirements

### Open Government Licence v3.0 (data.gov.uk datasets)

> Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government
> Licence v3.0.

Include the statement above (or a link to
https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/)
wherever you republish these catalogs.

### Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY)

Attribute the original publisher named in the `publisher` column of each
dataset row.

### US Public Domain (17 U.S.C. 105)

No copyright restriction in the United States. No attribution legally
required, though crediting the source agency is good practice.

Rows labeled `license_source = inferred-federal` were classified by
authorship (federal agency publisher or federal domain), not by an explicit
license field in the source API. The underlying legal basis is 17 U.S.C. 105
(works of US federal government employees). Contractor-produced data is
excluded from this inference by design.
